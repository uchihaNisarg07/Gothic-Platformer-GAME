# Game Design Document (GDD)

## 1. Game Overview
- **Genre:** 2D action-platformer.
- **Core concept:** A fast-paced platformer set in a ruined gothic castle, where a lone warrior fights simple enemies and traverses basic obstacles while uncovering the truth behind a fallen civilization.
- **Inspirations:** *Dead Cells* and *Mario* (art style body movements inspired by dead cells ie fast and fluid , obstacle level clearing inspired from mario).

## 2. Core Player Experience
- Fast, responsive movement.
- Simple, satisfying platforming.
- Minimal sword combat integrated into movement.
- A mysterious, moody gothic atmosphere.
- A clear path through the level (with obstacles + enemies (bots)) leading to an endpoint.

## 3. Core Gameplay Loop
- Jump over gaps and obstacles.
- Dash or move quickly through some sections.
- jump + dash or other combinations for a few obstacles
- Slash simple enemies with the sword.g
- checkpoints
- Continue to the level's endpoint.

## 4. Player Character
- **Role:** Lone warrior.
- **Weapon:** Sword.
- **Background:** The warrior's past is unknown.
- **Motivation:** Discover what caused the catastrophe and what happened to the fallen civilization.
- **abilities:** Move, jump, dash, and perform one basic sword slash.

## 5. Movement
- Movement -> feel fast and responsive.
- basic horizontal movement and jumping (+ vertical).
- simple dash
- responsive controls over complex movement systems.

## 6. Combat
- The player has one basic sword-slash attack.
- successful slash (1 or multiple) defeats a basic enemy.
- slash can be combined with dash

## 7. Obstacles and Platforming
- simple platforming challenges.
- complicated levels gradually to keep the learning curve lil steep
- assets/feedback such that hazards are interpretable by the player

## 8. Level Design
- **Structure:** One linear path leading to an endpoint.
- **Setting:** A gothic castle.
- uses a small number of platforms, obstacles, and enemy encounters.
- initial levels short enough for a beginner to build and test. (no tutorial)

## 9. World and Atmosphere
- **Setting:** A ruined dark-fantasy kingdom, represented in the prototype by a gothic castle.
- **Atmosphere:** Mysterious and moody.
- **World history:** A once-great civilization collapsed after a mysterious catastrophe.
- **Remnants:** Abandoned structures and traces of the former civilization remain.

## 10. Story
- The kingdom was destroyed by a mysterious catastrophe.
- A lone warrior explores its ruins to discover what happened.
- The warrior's past and connection to the catastrophe are unknown.
- The story can be conveyed lightly through the setting and environmental details.
- Detailed dialogue, cutscenes, and a fully developed narrative will be conveyed

## 11. Art Direction
- **Preferred style:** Detailed pixel art inspired by *Dead Cells*.
- **Environment:** Gothic castle architecture and a dark, atmospheric palette.

## 13. Audio
- simple sound effects for jumping, sword attacks, enemy defeat, death, and checkpoints and etc.
- atmospheric background music
